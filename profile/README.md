## Der rote Faden

*Der rote Faden*, the red thread, is the German idiom for the line that runs through everything and holds it together. Our mark shows it literally: a ball wound from a single thread. Where the grey yarn ends, the thread turns red and finds its way out. Every company we build or back is one section of that thread.
